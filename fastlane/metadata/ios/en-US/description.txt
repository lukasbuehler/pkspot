Find your next Parkour Spot, see what it's like before you go, and discover local Events and communities. Whether you're training close to home or exploring somewhere new, PK Spot helps you decide where to go next.

Find your next Spot
Explore the map for Parkour and Freerunning Spots near home or wherever you travel. Use filters to narrow your search and save the places you want to try.

Know before you go
Browse photos, ratings and practical details such as shelter, lighting and drinking water. Check the weather at a Spot to help plan your session.

Discover Events and jams
Find Parkour Events, explore the details and mark the ones you're interested in or going to.

Connect with your community
Discover local communities and follow the ones that matter to you. Add Spots, contribute photos and help keep information useful for the next person.

Keep your training story
Keep a private log of your sessions with notes, activity graphs and recovery pauses. Look back at where and how you've trained, at your own pace.

Built with the community
PK Spot is open source and shaped by the people who use it. Started in Zurich, built with the Parkour community.
